Privacy Policy

Sourdough Calculator · Last updated: 22 May 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile application Sourdough Calculator ("the app") handles information. The app is provided by an individual developer ("we", "us"). By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

Information the app collects

The app itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. It has no user accounts, no login, and no sign-up. All calculations are performed on your device. We do not have a server that receives your data, and we do not run our own analytics.

Advertising and third-party data collection

The app displays advertisements through Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. To deliver and measure ads, AdMob and Google may automatically collect certain information through the app, which may include:

  • A device advertising identifier (Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate, non-precise location derived from it
  • Device information, such as device model and operating system version
  • App usage and ad interaction data, such as ads viewed or tapped

This information is collected directly by Google, not by us, and is used to show ads (which may be personalized), to measure ad performance, and to detect fraud and abuse. We do not receive or have access to this personal data; we only see aggregated, non-identifying reports about ad revenue.

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Your choices

You can control ad personalization and limit ad tracking through your device settings. On most Android devices this is found under Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising. Note that ads may still be shown, but they will be less relevant to you.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so it can be addressed.

Data retention

Because the app does not collect or store personal data itself, there is no app-held data to retain or delete. Any data collected by Google AdMob is retained and managed by Google in accordance with Google's policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after a change indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
